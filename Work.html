The Brief

Pal’ Norte, meaning “to the north,” is a play on words that reflects a journey. For generations, “going north” has symbolized migration, sacrifice, hope, and the pursuit of opportunity. Yet the term is often reduced to politics, stereotypes, or statistics. This project seeks to reclaim and reframe that narrative, transforming it into something warm, celebratory, and human. At its core, Pal’ Norte is about honoring the immigrant journey through food, culture, and community.

Noval

Reignite your spark to read


The Brief

Noval is a social book tracking app designed to keep readers motivated and engaged. By turning reading into a rewarding habit, Noval helps users stay consistent through daily streaks, personalized insights, and detailed statistics that reveal reading patterns over time. Which allows users to celebrate progress every step of the way. Noval also brings readers together through its community feature, allowing friends to share updates, compare progress, and encourage one another while reading the same book or exploring new genres. With Noval, we regnite your spark to read.
