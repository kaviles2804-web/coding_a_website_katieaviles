About Me

Biography

I am a current senior at North Central College located in Naperville, IL. At NCC, I am obtaining my Bachelors degree in Graphic Design! Before North Central College, I attended the College of DuPage where I received my associate’s of arts degree with a concentration in Graphic Design. As a first generation college student, having an education is my biggest dream in life. I gain my strive and passion through my families support and my love for this career. My design practice is based on the belief that design should feel as much as it functions. Through branding, social media, and marketing I explore the space where strategy meets play. My work is rooted in intention but driven by curiosity, blending thoughtful concepts with experimental visuals. I create identites and digital experiences that are engaging, expressive, and built to connect.
